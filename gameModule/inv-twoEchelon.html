Game Background

Style Co. Ltd sells T-shirts through a central warehouse and three retailers (MK, WC, TST). Each retailer requests stock from the warehouse, which in turn orders from the supplier.

As the Purchasing Manager, your goal is to minimize total costs and maximize profit by deciding when and how much to order.

Key Parameters

🛒 Purchase: $25/unit + $150 transport per supplier order

🚚 Warehouse → Retailer: $60 per dispatch

💰 Selling price: $39/unit

⏱ Supplier lead time: 14 days

⏱ Retailer lead time: 3 days

🏢 Warehouse storage: $2.15/unit/year

🏪 Retailer storage: $3.88/unit/year

📦 Max supplier order: 9,999 units

Note: You can only place a new order when the previous order has fully arrived at that location.
