About the comparison models and hyperparameters
• Moving Average (MA): window sizes of 5 and 10 days.
• Simple Exponential Smoothing (SES): smoothing factor α (0–1). Forecast is next-day = current smoothed level L_t, with L_t = α·y_t + (1−α)·L_{t−1}.
• ARIMA(p,d,q): we expose p (autoregressive order) and d (difference order). For learning simplicity here we set q=0 and fit AR(p) on the d-th differenced series via least squares at each step. One-step forecast is inverted back to the original scale. Defaults: p=1, d=1.

Settings

q is fixed to 0 in this game for clarity.
Metrics: MAE = mean absolute error, RMSE = root mean square error, MAPE = mean absolute percentage error (safe denominator to avoid divide-by-zero).
